## The view

The organizing principle is that love and beauty belong first to God. He is "the Most Forgiving, the Most Loving" (*al-Wadud*).<Cite source="quran-haleem" locator="85:14" /> God loves those who follow His messenger,<Cite source="quran-haleem" locator="3:31" /> and the believers are people "He loves and who love Him."<Cite source="quran-haleem" locator="5:54" /> Spouses are bound by "love and kindness" that God ordains,<Cite source="quran-haleem" locator="30:21" /> and faith requires loving for one's brother what one loves for oneself.<Cite source="bukhari-sahih-khan" locator="13" />

Beauty is in God's work. He "gave everything its perfect form,"<Cite source="quran-haleem" locator="32:7" /> and there is no flaw in what He creates.<Cite source="quran-haleem" locator="67:3–4" /> People find beauty even in their herds coming home to rest,<Cite source="quran-haleem" locator="16:6" /> and the adornment God provides is not forbidden.<Cite source="quran-haleem" locator="7:31–32" /> A sound hadith says that God is beautiful and loves beauty; the registered translation renders the word as "Graceful."<Cite source="muslim-sahih-siddiqui" locator="91a" />

Al-Ghazali explains love as the soul's inclination toward what it finds pleasant and beautiful. Besides the outward senses there is an inner sense that perceives moral and divine beauty, and all lovable things are rightly loved as reflections of God.<Cite source="ghazali-alchemy-field" locator="ch. VIII, pp. 100–103" />

## What this explains well

The account explains why love and beauty feel like gifts that point beyond themselves, and it joins aesthetic delight, marital love and neighbour-love under one source.

## Christian response

The Christian shares the conviction that God is loving and the source of beauty. The difference is how God's love is shown: Christians hold that it is displayed in God giving his Son.<Cite source="bible-esv" locator="1 John 4:9–10" /> That is a revelatory disagreement.

## Pressure questions

- If God is the Most Loving, is love part of what God is, or one of His acts?
- Why would an inner sense of divine beauty need guidance by revelation?

## Deep dive

#### Love as real love

Al-Ghazali notes that some theologians denied that humans can truly love God, reducing love to obedience. He rejects this: love of God is real love, its highest form.<Cite source="ghazali-alchemy-field" locator="ch. VIII, p. 100" /> The *Alchemy* survives in an abridged English rendering of an Urdu version, so it is paraphrased here.

#### Music

Al-Ghazali holds that music and song stir whatever is already in the heart: in a heart full of love for God they kindle devotion, and in a heart full of base desire they feed it. He calls this a hidden fire that the music fans.<Cite source="ghazali-alchemy-field" locator="ch. V, pp. 64–66" /> The legal status of music is contested among Muslim jurists, and al-Ghazali's conditional approval is one position. Islamic visual art and calligraphy are not covered here.
