# Naturalistic atheism: final corpus audit

All 28 answers were read in full, in question order. **Result: 0 BLOCKER, 2 IMPORTANT, 6 MINOR.**

## 1. Overall coherence

The lane reads as one recognizable family. Its shared core:

- no supernatural order;
- inquiry continuous with the sciences;
- humans as evolved social animals;
- values without cosmic purpose;
- mortalism;
- history without a plot.

Disagreements inside the family are kept visible instead of being fused into one system:

- Humean, non-Humean and antirealist views of law (`order`, `induction`);
- realism, conventionalism, revisability and a posteriori views of logic (`logic-binding`);
- Railton's realism versus Mackie's error theory (`know-the-good`, `evil`, `great-and-terrible`);
- subjectivist, objectivist and nihilist accounts of meaning (`why-alive`);
- Epicurean versus deprivation accounts of death (`death`).

The lane never presents physicalism, Humeanism, scientism, moral anti-realism or nominalism as naturalism as such:

- `ultimate-reality`: "physicalism treated as the majority view, not the definition";
- `logic-binding`: "Not nominalism by default";
- `know-the-good`: Mackie is "an important alternative, not the definition of naturalism";
- `what-is-man`: "Atheism does not entail physicalism".

**Audit 8 (naturalism) passes.**

## 2. Source completeness

There is no canon, and none is manufactured. Representative voices:

- Hume (17 answers) and Russell (17);
- Darwin (11) and Railton (8);
- Carroll, Quine (through SEP), Mackie and Dennett;
- Martin, Ehrman and Lowder in Revelation & History.

The weakness lies in the distribution, not in fairness. **116 of the lane's 371 citations (31%) go to SEP entries.** On the evidence these maps are used accurately. Still, no contemporary naturalist metaphysician is read directly: not Oppy (blocked), Armstrong, Lewis or Papineau. Quine is quoted only as SEP quotes him (NAT-01).

## Diagnosis and remedy

- **Diagnosis:** plural and modest. Ignorance, suffering, conflicting motives, limited sympathy, harmful arrangements and finitude, with no guilt before God.
- **Remedy:** knowledge, care, cooperation, institutions, forgiveness and repair, all fallible and revisable.
- **Coherence:** the remedy is proportionate to the diagnosis and openly admits its limit, namely irreparable loss under mortalism (`self-salvation`, `guilt`, `final-end`). It is coherent. The Christian comparison correctly frames the limit as an adequacy question, not a contradiction.
