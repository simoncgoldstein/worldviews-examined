# Argument quality: final corpus audit

Every Christian response, strongest objection, Christian reply and pressure question was read for argument classification.

## 1. Corpus-wide result

The audit script counts argument-strength vocabulary in answer bodies (`data/INVENTORY.md`, "Argument-strength and provenance language"):

- "contradict…" and "incoheren…" occur almost entirely in disclaimers such as "not a contradiction" and "not a charge of incoherence".
- "cannot account / cannot ground" occurs rarely, and is always attributed (Van Til) or bounded.

**No answer calls a position a contradiction where it demonstrates only tension, incompleteness or disagreement.** This is a major achievement of the domain reviews. Typical correct classifications:

- "explanatory incompleteness, not contradiction" (naturalism `induction`, `one-and-many`; Hindu `induction`);
- "internal tension, not a contradiction" (Hindu `knowledge-possible`; Buddhist `logic-binding`, `induction`);
- "underdetermination, not a contradiction" (naturalism `worship`; Islam flagship);
- "theological disagreement grounded in competing revelations" (Judaism, Islam, Hindu UR and Man);
- "comparative explanatory adequacy, not a proof" (Christian UR and KT replies).

The other failure patterns:

| Failure pattern | Result |
|---|---|
| "Cannot account for" where only incompleteness is shown | Not found. Van Til's "only Christian theism can account" is attributed and bounded (`logic-binding`, `induction`). |
| Christian premise used as if neutral | Mostly flagged ("rests on theistic premises"). Residual cases are in the pressure questions (XQ-04). |
| Diversity treated as refutation | Explicitly disclaimed in every lane. Residual: Hindu `ultimate-authority` PQ3. |
| Refusal of a premise treated as evasion | Not found. The Buddhist `something-rather-than-nothing` response, "the disagreement is about whether the question is legitimate", is exemplary. |
| Later chronology treated as refutation | Not found; explicitly avoided (`islam/jesus`; `buddhism/revelation`). |
| A question the opposing view has already answered in its own terms | **Found:** ISL-01, HIN-01, HIN-04, HIN-05 and the XQ-04 cluster. |

**Christian claims examined as critically as competing claims.** Yes. The Christian strongest objections are serious, drawn from real critics (Kant, John Taylor, Hume's Philo, Carroll, Ehrman, Lowder, Maimonides, Vasubandhu, Russell, Darwin). The replies concede limits:

- "Belief in God's faithfulness does not escape the circle";
- "Philo's challenge remains a serious dispute";
- "The account rests on God's just judgment and free mercy, not on a proof that grace is owed equally".

If anything, the Morality & Evil and Salvation & Destiny Christian replies concede *too much*, leaving the Christian position under-argued (CHR-04 to CHR-07).
