# Hindu traditions: final corpus audit

All 28 answers were read in full, in question order. **Result: 0 BLOCKER, 3 IMPORTANT, 4 MINOR.**

## 1. Overall coherence

This is the most consistently school-differentiated lane on the site. Every answer keeps Advaita (Śaṅkara), Viśiṣṭādvaita (Rāmānuja) and Dvaita (Madhva) distinct. Nyāya (Udayana, the Nyāya Sūtra), Mīmāṃsā (Kumārila), Yoga and Cārvāka enter where the question requires them.

Vertical consistency holds across these topics:

- Brahman and the three schools;
- the self (eternal *ātman* versus real dependent souls versus graded souls);
- the world (names and forms, Brahman's body, eternally distinct realities);
- karma and the Lord's dispensation;
- *śruti* authority;
- *mokṣa* as nondual realization or as dependent bliss;
- cyclical history.

**No silent move from "Advaita teaches" to "Hinduism teaches" was found.** Theses and views name the schools throughout.

## 4. Internal diversity

Vedānta diversity is excellent. Its sensitive points are disclosed rather than hidden: Madhva's eternally bound souls, Śaṅkara on śūdras, and Manu's gender provisions.

Devotional and Śaiva diversity is thin (HIN-02):

- Bhakti is carried by Rāmānuja alone.
- There is no Bhāgavata Purāṇa (SOURCES.md lists it "when relevant to bhakti traditions"), no Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism, no Śaiva Siddhānta and no Kashmir Śaivism.
- Abhinavagupta is registered as the aesthetics specialist and never used.
- This follows the SOURCES.md centre of gravity (Vedānta), but `worship`, `love-beauty-creativity` and `final-end` are exactly the questions where it matters.

## 9. Argument-quality concerns

Classification is excellent throughout ("internal tension, not contradiction"; "explanatory incompleteness, not error"; "history cannot confirm it either"). Concerns:

- **HIN-01 (IMPORTANT).** The worship critique is made without the opponent's own theology of images.
- **HIN-04.** `ultimate-reality` PQ1 ("whose is the ignorance…") is Rāmānuja's fair intra-Vedānta objection. Advaita's classical replies are never given: Vācaspati's Bhāmatī (the *jīva* as locus), the Vivaraṇa (Brahman as locus), and *avidyā* as *anirvacanīya*.
- **HIN-05.** The `suffering` and `evil` pressure questions ("What grounds the karmic order rather than merely naming the connection?") do not engage the theistic Vedānta answer: the Lord as dispenser of fruits, BS III.2.38 *phalam ata upapatteḥ*, already reflected in `order`.
- **`ultimate-authority` PQ3** ("If the same texts yield three incompatible readings, what decides between them?") reintroduces diversity as pressure after the response has rightly said diversity refutes none of the schools, and it applies equally to Protestant hermeneutics (XQ-04).

## Diagnosis and remedy

- **Diagnosis:**
  - common to all schools: bondage in *saṃsāra* through desire, ignorance and karma;
  - Advaita: root superimposition (*adhyāsa*);
  - Rāmānuja: karma contracting the soul's knowledge, with real agency and divine displeasure;
  - Madhva: dependent souls with innate grades.
- **Remedy:** discipline plus liberating knowledge (Advaita); devotion, refuge and the Lord's favour (Rāmānuja); grace (Madhva).
- **Final state:** nondual realization, or the dependent bliss of a distinct soul with no return.
- **Coherence:** high within each school. The tension the corpus identifies is in Advaita: ultimate culpability for another's injury when individuality is not ultimate (`guilt` PQ). It is classified correctly.
- **The comparison the corpus supports:** Rāmānuja's "demanding no price" (Gītā 18.66) against Reformed satisfaction. The flagship draws it well; `guilt` assumes it.
