## Assessments requested by the brief

**Flagship biblical grounding.** The view now follows the biblical storyline: creation and image (Genesis 2:7; 1:26–28; Psalm 8), Fall (Genesis 3; Romans 5:12, 15–19), corruption (Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 3:10–18; Ephesians 2:1–3), preserved dignity (Genesis 9:6; James 3:9), kindness to all (Matthew 5:45; Acts 14:17), and renewal (Colossians 3:10). Scripture supplies the order of the argument, not decoration. No passage is made to carry more than its context allows: Romans 3 is synthesized as Paul's catena; Acts 14:17 is kept to God's witness of kindness, with common grace now marked as the doctrine built on it (M-5).

**Theologian/confession balance.** The Westminster Confession appears twice in the view, both times as confirmation after Scripture. Calvin explains the gifts and their corruption (II.2.12, 15; II.3.3), which matches the biblical account of preserved dignity. Augustine's disordered love explains the will's direction under Jeremiah 17:9 without replacing it. In the reply, Edwards's natural/moral inability is quoted exactly ("wholly excuses" / "never can excuse"). Total depravity is total in extent, not maximal (WCF 6.4 glossed by Larger Catechism Q. 25 and WCF 16.7). WCF 9.3 keeps the distinction between natural and spiritual ability.

**Flagship length: some compression warranted.** It was 1,698 inventory words at the start of this review (994 visible / 691 Deep dive), against 1,011–1,141 for the other five flagships, whose visible text is about 500–560 words. The extra visible length is the Scripture-first storyline that CHR-02 required. Every paragraph is a step in that history, and cutting it would cut Scripture. The Deep dive had duplication (M-6), which was removed. The fixes I-1, M-3 and M-5 added about 40 visible words. The page is now 1,686 words (1,035 / 638), and the elementary answer is complete by the end of the view. No further compression is recommended without loss of argument.

**Adam's biblical and confessional position.** The section now says that the Standards *presuppose* "a real first man who acted as covenant head, whose transgression stands in representative relation to his descendants as Christ's obedience stands to his people". This matches LC 22 ("a public person"), WCF 7.2 (covenant of works; "in him to his posterity"; quoted exactly) and WCF 6.3 (imputed guilt and conveyed corruption), and it does not exaggerate their wording. The modern phrase "historical Adam" no longer appears. Federal headship matches R1's `guilt`, which uses the same Romans 5 two-heads pattern and Witsius's covenant parallel. No seventeenth-century theory of imputation is equated with the Standards' wording: the immediate/mediate dispute is reported, attributed to Turretin and Placaeus, and kept separate from the confessional sentence.

**The objection and reply.** Kant's and Taylor's objection is unchanged and strong. The reply has three parts. Edwards answers the "misfortune" horn. Edwards's argument about the first sin answers Taylor. Imputation is grounded, without apology, in revelation and in the Adam/Christ parallel. The decree is now stated at confessional strength (I-1). Civil virtue is expected under WCF 9.3 and Calvin II.3.4. The single remaining concession, on testability, concerns how far the claim can be measured, not whether the doctrine is held.

**Common grace and civil good** are coherent with `knowledge-possible`, `fail-the-good` and `self-salvation`. Dort III/IV.4 ("do not use it rightly even in matters of nature and society") and the flagship's "frame just laws" are reconciled by WCF 16.7, which the flagship quotes.

- **Prose.** The image paragraph is now doctrine-first: creature fitted to know God, reflect his goodness and represent his rule. It gives two negations (not God's essence, not bodily likeness) and the intellectual, moral and vocational content, and only then attributes Calvin and Bavinck.

The intermediate state is real (WCF 32.1, quoted exactly: "waiting for the full redemption of their bodies"), and the resurrection is the final hope. R9's consciousness research was not absorbed.
