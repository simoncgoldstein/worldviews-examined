**Is one lane held to a stricter evidentiary standard?** No systematic asymmetry was found. Christianity's own historical claims (`revelation`, `jesus`) face the same four-level test as Sinai, the Qur'an, the Veda and the Mahāyāna sūtras. In some places Christianity is held to a stricter standard than its rivals (it concedes the induction circle; its prophecy argument is downgraded).

**Does the Christian response answer the strongest version actually presented?** Yes in about 150 of 168 answers. The exceptions are the classical answers omitted: ISL-01 (*taḥrīf*), HIN-01 (image theology), HIN-04 (the locus of *avidyā*) and HIN-05 (karma's grounding).

## 8. Historical accountability (Audits 16 and 19)

**Pages with significant historical claims:** all `revelation`, `history` and `jesus` answers; `judaism/ultimate-authority` (Sinai); `islam/ultimate-authority`; `christianity/great-and-terrible` (Adam); `naturalism/what-is-man` (Darwin's rankings).

- **Four-level distinction** (source claim, historical conclusion, explanatory inference, theological conclusion): respected throughout Revelation & History. Examples:
  - "to judge from the tradition Paul says he had received";
  - "The test dates the parchment, not the writing";
  - "That makes the claim testable, not proven";
  - "A revelation re-found in every cycle cannot be refuted by history, but for that reason history cannot confirm it either."
- **Earlier domains:** no historical claim written before the standard was found to overstate. The flagship's historicity-of-Adam sentence is a confessional rather than evidential problem (CHR-03).
- **Wording strength:**
  - strongly established, worded strongly: the crucifixion under Pilate; Jesus existed;
  - well supported: early proclamation within years (hedged);
  - debated: empty tomb ("genuinely debated"), Testimonium ("scholars debate"), Daniel's date ("critical scholarship disputes");
  - theological inference: resurrection as best explanation ("The Christian argument is…").

  All appropriately worded.
- **Chronology-alone refutation:** explicitly avoided (`islam/jesus`: "This is not the claim that later means false").
