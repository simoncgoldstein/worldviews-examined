## History pattern by worldview

| Lane | Pattern | Goal | Final horizon | Key distinction kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reformed | Linear, Christ-centred, providential | God's purpose to unite all things in Christ | Resurrection, judgement, renewed creation (re-creation, Bavinck) | Augustine's two cities mixed to the end: no utopia, no chaos; no millennial chart |
| Naturalism | Physical arrow of time without purpose; contingent progress | None built in; human-chosen aims | An end (Russell's "vast death of the solar system"), not a completion | Not "meaningless"; progress real but "no invariable rule"; Marx flagged as one theory, not representative |
| Judaism | Linear, covenantal: exile to redemption | Messianic restoration, then the World to Come | Messianic age (this world), resurrection, World to Come kept distinct | Maimonides' natural messianic age as a major account, with the Sages' controversy, apocalyptic strands, the 6,000-year teaching and the curse on calculators |
| Islam | One line of sacred history to the Hour | Resurrection and judgement | Cosmic dissolution and recreation; all perishes but God's Face | Rise and fall of peoples (3:137; 13:11; 7:34) and Ibn Khaldun's dynasties *inside* the linear frame; "Islam does not teach that history as a whole is cyclical" |
| Hindu | Beginningless cycles of kalpas and yugas | No cosmic goal; dharma restored in each age | Kalki restores a new Kṛta age; liberation from the cycle | "Kalki renews the cycle; he does not end it"; history neither meaningless nor illusory |
| Buddhist | Beginningless saṃsāra; contraction and expansion | No cosmic goal; Buddhas recur | No consummation; liberation ends rebirth | Future hope exists (DN 26, Metteyya) but "no final cosmic consummation" |

## Corrections and decisions during production

- **Arrow of time:** the dossier cited "Carroll (UR, REUSE)" for thermodynamics and heat death, but no read passage
  supported it. A narrow check found Carroll §1 n. 1 (low-entropy past grounds the causal asymmetry); "heat death" was
  dropped and Russell's 1903 sentence carries the cosmic future, attributed as his.
- **Darwin ch. V** ("progress is no invariable rule") was read in Gutenberg #2300 and cited by chapter only; Darwin's
  Victorian racial rankings are noted as rejected by later naturalists.
- **Saadia VIII.8:** an early draft listed specific "proofs"; it was replaced by the general statement the dossier
  supports.
- **Thinker metadata:** the Islam draft listed al-Ghazali, who does not appear; Ibn Khaldun was profiled instead (bio from
  de Slane's introduction, pp. iii–v). The Buddhist draft needed a genuine Theravāda voice; Buddhaghosa's
  *Visuddhimagga* XIII was located in a cached copy of the registered edition.
- **Repetition:** Maimonides' Kings 11:4 test was quoted in both Jewish `history` and `jesus`; the `history` Deep dive now
  paraphrases it and links to `jesus`.
