## Organizing principle by lane

- **christianity:** love, beauty and making come from God (who is love, beautiful, maker) and are good when ordered to him (Augustine's "order of love").
- **naturalism:** evolved capacities amplified by culture; origin ≠ value; Hume's standard of taste.
- **judaism:** imitation of God and service through commandments (beautified mitzvot; Bezalel; majestic Adam).
- **islam:** love and beauty belong first to God (al-Wadud; God is beautiful and loves beauty); al-Ghazali's inner sense of beauty.
- **hinduism:** bhakti and the divine source of glory and bliss (Gītā 10.41; Taittirīya II.7), with Advaita/Rāmānuja readings of love.
- **buddhism:** clinging vs purified mind (mettā vs near enemy; beauty's gratification, drawback, escape).
