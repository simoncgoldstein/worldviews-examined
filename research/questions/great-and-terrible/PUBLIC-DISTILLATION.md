# Public distillation record

The six published answers (`src/content/answers/*/great-and-terrible.mdx`) were condensed in a
single-agent editorial pass after Phase 3 review (2026-10-05). The page fell from about 11,000 to
about 6,400 words and from 405 to 223 citation markers. Nothing was deleted from the research
record: the fuller pre-distillation answers are in `drafts/` and in git history (`a0adca6`), and the
packets keep every claim-to-citation row.

**Rules followed.**

- No new research.
- No new quotation: every quotation in the condensed answers appears in the reviewed text at
  `a0adca6`.
- No new (source, locator) pair in any answer: every remaining citation was verified in Phase 3.
- Codex findings R-1 to R-12 were preserved in substance.

Each worldview below lists:

- the thinkers removed from public presentation (their profiles and sources remain);
- the material that remains in the dossier only.
