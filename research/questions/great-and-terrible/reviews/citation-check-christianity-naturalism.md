## Non-SUPPORTS items and fixes

- C#19 (edwards-works-hickman-1 Freedom of the Will I.4, PARTIAL): Moral-inability quotes exact (I.4). But the claim that natural inability excuses is not in I.4 (it is in III.4: 'Natural Inability... wholly excuses'), and 'ought implies can' is not Edwards's wording. Fix: cite III.4 for the excuse clause; drop quote marks on ought implies can.
- N#10 (wrangham-two-types Abstract, PARTIAL): Abstract gives high proactive / low reactive aggression but not 'within the group', 'planned' or 'coalitionary' (body has 'purposeful planned attack', coalitions of males, 'low frequency of within-group fighting'). Fix: locator 'Abstract; Introduction'.
- N#13 (railton-moral-realism pp. 193-194, PARTIAL): Quote exact (p.194). Slavery is not discussed on pp.193-194; Railton mentions slavery on p.195 (plantation slavery lasted centuries) and p.198 and never discusses its abolition. Fix: drop 'such as the abolition of slavery', or say 'real but uneven'.
- N#38 (wrangham-two-types Abstract, PARTIAL): Low severity. Abstract names the 'Hobbes-Huxley' and 'Rousseau-Kropotkin' positions; 'both partly correct' is exact. Fix if desired: say 'Hobbesian and Rousseauian positions'.
- N#45 (dennett-self-artifact p. 48, QUOTE INACCURATE): Dennett p.48: 'the practice of asking, and giving, reasons' (commas). Fix: write "asking, and giving, reasons" or drop the quote marks. Text via Zenodo 915272 PDF.
- N#57 (darwin-descent ch. IV, p. 123, PARTIAL): p.123 says humanity to animals is a late moral acquisition 'unfelt by savages'; 'savages' appears but the explicit ranking in moral development is inference from pp.121-123. Fix: cite pp. 122-123 and phrase as 'treats humanity to animals as unfelt by savages and a late moral acquisition'.

- The partial items are mild overstatements, not fabrications.
