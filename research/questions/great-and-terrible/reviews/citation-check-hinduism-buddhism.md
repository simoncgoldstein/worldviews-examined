# Citation check: Hinduism and Buddhism, great-and-terrible

Verified 2026-10-05. Report only; no content files edited.

Method notes:
- Archive.org `_djvu.txt` full texts were used for Sastri (Śaṅkara Gītā), Govindacharya (Rāmānuja Gītā), Thibaut (SBE 34, 48), Müller (SBE 1), Subba Rau, Vidyabhusana, Jha and Woods. OCR is noisy, so wording was matched on normalised text.
- Sujato texts came from the SuttaCentral bilarasuttas API.
- Path of Purification came from the BPS/AccessToInsight PDF.
- IEP and SEP pages are the current snapshots.
- Shantideva, Vasubandhu, Ratnagotravibhāga and Nāgārjuna were checked against the GRETIL Sanskrit, not the print translations in sources.yaml (Crosby/Skilton, Garfield, Johnston). Verse numbers and sense match. English quotation wording could not be checked for these.
- Thibaut page numbers were spot-checked by running heads only. Content matches the cited sutra in every case.
- Dōgen (Tanahashi, print) has no accessible text, so those items are COULD NOT CHECK.

Totals: Hinduism 54 cites: 48 SUPPORTS, 4 PARTIAL, 2 QUOTE INACCURATE. Buddhism 72 cites: 64 SUPPORTS, 5 PARTIAL, 3 COULD NOT CHECK. Grand total: 126 cites, 112 SUPPORTS, 9 PARTIAL, 2 QUOTE INACCURATE, 3 COULD NOT CHECK, 0 DOES NOT SUPPORT.
