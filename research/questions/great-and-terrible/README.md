# Research dossier: `great-and-terrible`

published answers in `src/content/answers/*/great-and-terrible.mdx`. It is kept so later agents can
reuse verified work and the method, not as publishable text.

## Unresolved scholarly issues (documented, not hidden)

- m. Sanhedrin 4:5: the "from Israel" reading versus the universal reading is disclosed; manuscript priority is not adjudicated.
- Madhva's doctrine of eternally different soul-types is contested in scholarship; the answer presents it as Madhva's and as controversial.
- Whether *prapatti* (surrender) belongs to Rāmānuja himself is disputed; disclosed.
- Readings of the "luminous mind" passage and whether buddha-nature is a kind of self are contested; Dōgen is cited from a Japanese web text whose base edition is unstated.
- Interpretations of al-Ghazali on human choice (Griffel's determinist reading) and of al-Maturidi on unaided moral reason differ; attributed by name.
- The historicity of Adam is debated among contemporary Reformed scholars; federal headship is presented as the Confession's position.
- Whether responsibility requires self-origination, and whether it is compatible with a comprehensive decree, is left open in the Christian reply as a disputed ultimate premise.
