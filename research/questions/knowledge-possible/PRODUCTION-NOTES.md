## Framing decisions

- **Question separation.** Every answer explains *how a mind comes to know*: faculties, perception, inference, testimony, error and correction, and limits. Scripture appears only where a lane treats it as a means of knowledge (Śaṅkara's pramāṇas; testimony at Sinai) or as the corrective that `ultimate-authority` takes up. Each answer links forward to `ultimate-authority` rather than arguing final adjudication here.
- **Reformed.** Same Maker of knower and known (Prov 3:19, 20:12; Gen 1:26–27; Bavinck's one Logos) → real but creaturely knowledge (Ps 147:5; WCF 2.2; 1 Cor 13:12) → sin as suppression, not loss of reason (Rom 1:18–21; Calvin II.2.12–16 with both the "shapeless ruin" and "could not be entirely destroyed") → Scripture as spectacles (Calvin I.6.1). Archetypal/ectypal, illumination vs. Bavinck's realism, and Van Til are in the Deep dive. Van Til's "nothing so absurd" is quoted so the page cannot be read as "unbelievers know nothing". The stronger transcendental claim is explicitly deferred to `logic-binding`.
- **Naturalism.** Evolved, corrigible faculties + public science → Quine (via SEP) → moderate naturalism. The Christian response concedes the replies to the reliability worry (Ramsey, Fodor, Fales, Millikan) and labels its claim as comparative explanatory adequacy. Plantinga appears only in the Deep dive, described as the Christian interlocutor; the naturalist replies are given in full. Darwin's "horrid doubt" is placed in its theistic context.
- **Judaism.** Wisdom as God's gift and reason commanded → Saadia's four roots and their tests → Sinai testimony (Deut 4:9–10; Kuzari I.25) → Saadia / Halevi / Maimonides as three emphases. Neither rationalism nor traditionalism.
- **Islam.** Faculties, *fiṭra*, signs → Nasafi's three sources → *ilhām* explicitly denied → al-Ghazali's crisis, with the light described as restoring the primary assumptions, not giving new doctrine. School differences on reason's reach in the Deep dive.
- **Hindu.** Pramāṇa counts → Nyāya definitions and realism → defeasibility and Mīmāṃsā intrinsic validity → Advaita's two levels → theistic Vedānta realism. Correspondence realism is not imposed on Advaita.
- **Buddhist.** Tested faith and direct realization (AN 3.65 in context; MN 95; MN 47; MN 38) → Dharmakīrti's two pramāṇas and non-deception → no enduring knower → Madhyamaka and Yogācāra as dissenting analyses. Not anti-reason; no Cartesian self.

## Self-review

- **Elementary-answer test.** Every thesis answers "why can we know?" directly, without thinker names. The Hindu thesis was rewritten from a description of the method ("schools analyse the means of knowing") to the answer itself.
- **Naturalness.** Each opening explains the mechanism before any thinker. Thinker names in the visible prose are attached to distinctive formulations (Saadia's roots, al-Ghazali's crisis, Kumārila's intrinsic validity, Dharmakīrti's definitions, Quine's naturalization).
- **Question separation.** Final adjudication is not argued; every lane links forward to `ultimate-authority`.
- **Source fidelity.** Scripture, Tanakh, Qur'an, sūtras and suttas carry the opening claims in their lanes; interpreters follow.
- **Internal diversity.** Reformed illumination vs. realism vs. Van Til; replacement vs. moderate naturalism and realism vs. its critics; Saadia/Halevi/Maimonides; Maturidi/Ash'ari/Ibn Taymiyya; Nyāya/Mīmāṃsā/Advaita/theistic Vedānta; canon/Dharmakīrti/Madhyamaka/Yogācāra.
- **Argument strength.** Every Christian evaluation is classified: comparative explanatory adequacy (naturalism, Buddhist), theological disagreement within a shared epistemology (Judaism, Islam), internal tension, not contradiction (Advaita).
- **Caveats.** Unbelievers know (Calvin, Van Til p. 120); Darwin's context; Quine via SEP; al-Ghazali's light ≠ *ilhām*; Advaita not correspondence realist; Dharmottara's gloss distinguished from Dharmakīrti's sūtra.
