## 2. Recommended Phase 4 order (by reuse and efficiency)

1. **Morality, Evil & the Human Problem.** 22 of 36 cells are strongly covered.
   - `fail-the-good` and `self-deception` are near-derivative of the flagship.
   - `know-the-good` and `evil` reuse its sources directly.
   - The only N cells are naturalism on suffering and on death.
2. **Salvation, Liberation & Human Destiny.** `self-salvation` and `guilt` were researched in depth for the Christian responses; `after-death` and `final-end` mostly need eschatology locators in checked editions.
3. **Man & Human Nature.** `what-is-man` is fully covered and `worship` nearly so; schedule `offspring-family` and `love-beauty-creativity` (concise questions) last within the domain.
4. **Ultimate Reality.** Every lane needs targeted metaphysics reading, but most of it sits in already-checked editions (BSBh, Śrī Bhāṣya, *Guide*, MMK, WCF). It is a prerequisite for the next two domains.
5. **Knowledge & Truth.** This needs the largest new source set:
   - Dharmakīrti;
   - al-Ghazali's *Munqidh*;
   - Saadia's introduction;
   - Nyāya *pramāṇa*;
   - naturalized epistemology;
   - and, crucially for the site's method, lawful access to Van Til and Bahnsen.
6. **Revelation & History.** This depends on `ultimate-authority` (domain 5). `jesus` needs new research in four lanes.

**The original expectation is confirmed in part.** Man & Human Nature is cheap at its core, but it is not the cheapest domain. Morality & Evil reuses more of Phase 3, because the flagship researched moral psychology and wrongdoing even more than anthropology as such.

## 7. Production and research policy (summary)

The canonical text is in `docs/METHODOLOGY.md` ("Phase 4 production and research policy") and `docs/SOURCES.md` ("Source priorities for production"). In short:

- **Phase 3 was a maximum-rigor calibration run.** Its roughly 30-agent workflow is not the production model.
- **Agents:** no subagents by default. Work in one focused session per bounded task. Use parallel chats only with narrow scopes, each starting from the existing dossiers.
- **Reuse first.** Begin with this map, the dossiers, the source registry and the thinker profiles. Do not rediscover or reread verified material unless a new claim needs it.
- **Stopping rule.** Stop researching once there is enough evidence to represent the prominent position accurately, disclose materially important internal differences, and support every published claim with a reliable locator.
- **Minimum sufficient corpus.** Seek the smallest high-quality source set per worldview and domain. Do not build bibliographies.
- **Review:**
  - routine derivative answers need no adversarial review;
  - newly researched domain batches may get one independent batch review;
  - anchor questions may get stronger review when warranted.
