## 1. Core account

Naturalism has no religious salvation doctrine or binding canon. Humans face limited sympathy, conflicting motives, ignorance, suffering and unjust institutions. Representative humanists seek improvement through knowledge, love, cooperation, education and institutions; outcomes remain fallible. Moral realism (Railton) and anti-realism (Mackie) differ, and neither should stand for the entire lane. Guilt may mean actual culpable wrongdoing or the feeling of remorse; they can come apart. Repair, apology, accountability and interpersonal forgiveness address relationships without a divine tribunal. Forgiveness need not make the act innocent, remove legal consequences or restore an unsafe relationship.

Representative mortalism says the person ceases at death. This is not an unconscious observer experiencing an infinite darkness. Psychological versus organismic theories of persistence complicate identity questions, but thought experiments about copying are not evidence of an afterlife. Flourishing and finite meaning are proposed goods; progress is an aim, not a guaranteed culmination. Naturalism alone does not settle every ethical value or technological possibility.

## 3. Question map

G: remorse → truthful acknowledgment/repair/accountability; victim's standing; distinguish present repair from undoing history. A: Russell's mortalism first, then the identity caveat; no claim every naturalist endorses animalism. F: finite flourishing and shared goods; no promised final destiny. S: personal and structural problems → revisable collective improvement → effort and evidence → no supernatural rescue or assurance → mortal projects.

## 6. Remaining gaps

Ready with narrow caveat for A: personal-identity theories do not uniformly entail immediate cessation at biological death; the public scope is representative mortalism without speculative uploading. G uses a scholarly map of secular forgiveness, not a claim that naturalism uniquely owns it. No primary therapeutic outcome evidence was sought, so make no efficacy statistics or clinical advice. No claim that secular repair is merely symptom suppression.
