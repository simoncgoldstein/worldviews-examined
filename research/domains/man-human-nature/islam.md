# Classical Islam — Man & Human Nature

Research packet, 2026-10-06. Codes: WM, WA, OF, LB, WO as in `christianity.md`. Status: P3, ME/SD, NEW, NEW SRC.

**Method.** Qur'an (Abdel Haleem, quran.com resource 85) → sound hadith (Bukhari, Muslim on sunnah.com) → al-Ghazali
as synthesizing voice → Ash'ari/Maturidi/Ibn Taymiyya only where they change the answer. No general Islamic law.

## 1. Core account

The human being is fashioned from clay and given God's breathed spirit, hearing, sight and minds (15:28–29; 32:7–9);
honoured above much of creation (17:70, P3), made a *khalifa* (2:30, P3), taught the names (2:31–33, P3), formed in
the best stature (95:4; 40:64; 64:3), born on the *fitra* (Bukhari 1385; 30:30, P3/ME). Al-Ghazali identifies the real
self with the heart, a subtle lordly reality also called spirit, soul and intellect (Marvels pp. 6–8, P3). Humanity
and jinn were created "only to worship Me," God needing nothing from them (51:56–57); life is a test (67:2, ME).
Spouses are a sign: created "from among yourselves" for tranquillity, with "love and kindness" ordained between
them (30:21); humanity spreads from one soul and its mate, and kinship ties are sacred (4:1); spouses, children and
grandchildren are blessings (16:72) and also attractions and tests (18:46; 64:14–15); prophets had wives and
offspring (13:38); monasticism was not ordained (57:27); the Prophet rejects vowed celibacy and commends marriage
(Bukhari 5063, 5065). Parents are honoured beside the worship of God (17:23–24; 46:15; 4:36). Love: believers love
God more ardently than idolaters love rivals (2:165); God loves those who follow the Prophet (3:31; 5:54) and is
*al-Wadud*, the Most Loving (85:14). Beauty: God "gave everything its perfect form" (32:7), shaped human forms well
(40:64; 64:3), placed beauty in livestock (16:6), permits adornment (7:31–32); God is beautiful (*jamil*) and loves
beauty (Muslim 91a). Worship is the human end and belongs to God alone (1:5; 2:21; 51:56); associating partners is
the gravest wrong (31:13; 4:48, SD); even intercessory intermediaries are rejected (39:3); Abraham reasons from
setting stars to the Creator (6:76–79); desire itself can become a god (45:23, P3); a willing being necessarily
worships something (Hoover p. 42, P3).

## 3. Question map

- **WM.** *Main:* clay and divine breath; honoured *khalifa*; heart as real self; *fitra*; tested. *Texts:* 15:28–29;
  32:7–9; 2:30–33; 17:70; 95:4; Bukhari 1385. *Thinkers:* al-Ghazali Marvels pp. 6–8, 25; Maturidi (Harvey p. 27)
  (P3). *Distinction:* *khalifa* as vicegerent vs successor (P3); Ibn Sina's rational soul (P3).
- **WA.** *Main:* to worship and know God; life a test; all of life can be worship; God needs nothing (51:57).
  *Texts:* 51:56–58; 67:2; 2:21; Bukhari 50 (*ihsan*); Bukhari 6502 (drawing near until God loves him). *Thinkers:*
  al-Ghazali Alchemy VIII p. 100 (love of God "the final aim"; human perfection). *Distinction:* whether God's acts
  have purposes—Ibn Taymiyya's wise purposes (*hikma*) (Hoover pp. 41–44) vs Ash'ari voluntarism (God acts as owner,
  SEP §5) (P3). *Caveat:* no direct Ash'ari text on *ta'lil* mined; state the contrast at the level of the existing
  locators.
- **OF.** *Main:* marriage a divine sign of tranquillity, love and mercy and the normative path of the prophets;
  children a blessing and a trust/test; parents honoured next to God's worship; celibacy not ordained. *Texts:*
  30:21; 4:1; 16:72; 13:38; 57:27; 18:46; 64:14–15; 25:74; 17:23–24; 46:15; 2:187; Bukhari 5063, 5065. *Thinker:*
  al-Ghazali Alchemy VII pp. 87–90 (marriage multiplies God's worshippers; children's intercession; companionship
  renews devotion; household care; patience with a wife as part of religion) and its disadvantages section.
  *Distinction:* al-Ghazali weighs marriage's advantages and hindrances for the devotee—marriage is not
  unconditionally superior for every person. *Caveat:* polygyny, divorce and gender-role law are outside scope;
  al-Ghazali's household roles reflect his setting.
- **LB.** *Main:* love is divine (al-Wadud) and reciprocal with obedience; spousal love and mercy ordained; neighbor
  love intrinsic to faith; God beautiful and loving beauty; the world without flaw. *Texts:* 2:165; 3:31; 5:54;
  85:14; 30:21; Bukhari 13; Muslim 91a; 32:7; 67:3–4; 16:6; 7:31–32. *Thinker:* al-Ghazali Alchemy VIII pp. 100–103
  (love = inclination to the pleasant; an inner sense perceives moral and divine beauty; all lovable things loved as
  reflections of God; causes of love beginning with self-love led to God) and V (music fans whatever love is already
  in the heart). *Distinctions:* (1) some theologians reduced love of God to obedience (Alchemy VIII p. 100); (2) music
  is contested among jurists—al-Ghazali's conditional approval is one position. *Caveat:* Siddiqui renders *jamil* in
  Muslim 91a as "Graceful"; paraphrase as "beautiful," noting translation.
- **WO.** *Main:* worship ('ibada) is the purpose of creation and due to God alone; *shirk* is the gravest wrong; the
  human is necessarily a worshipper, rightly or wrongly. *Texts:* 1:5; 51:56; 31:13; 112; 39:3; 6:76–79; 2:165; 45:23;
  Bukhari 50. *Thinkers:* Ibn Taymiyya via Hoover p. 42 (willing being necessarily has a god); al-Ghazali Alchemy
  VIII. *Distinction:* worship as ritual acts vs whole-life orientation (*ihsan*); veneration of saints contested
  between Sufi practice and Ibn Taymiyya (not researched—do not adjudicate).

## 5. Important distinctions

- Purpose and divine wisdom: Ibn Taymiyya/Maturidi vs Ash'ari voluntarism (WA).
- Love of God as real love vs reduction to obedience (LB).
- Music and arts: contested legal status; al-Ghazali conditional (LB).
- Marriage normative (sunna) vs al-Ghazali's case-by-case weighing for devotees (OF).

## 6. Remaining gaps

- *Ihya'* Books 12 and 36 in their full form: not lawfully readable; the *Alchemy* chapters are al-Ghazali's own
  abridgement and suffice. Ash'ari primary on divine purposes: not mined (caveat). Islamic visual art and calligraphy:
  out of scope.
