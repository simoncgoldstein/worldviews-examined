## How to use

1. Open the lane packet (`christianity.md`, `naturalism.md`, `judaism.md`, `islam.md`, `hinduism.md`,
   `buddhism.md`). Each has: (1) core account; (2) core texts checked (REUSE / NEW / NEW SRC, with "already have",
   "core works needing passages", "important missing core source"); (3) question map; (4) passage map; (5) important
   distinctions; (6) remaining gaps. The Christian packet adds the Reformed Scripture audit (§3a).
2. **P3** locators were citation-checked in the flagship; **ME/SD** in the Morality/Evil and Salvation/Destiny
   dossiers; **NEW** was read in this pass in the registered edition (or an accessible text with the same canonical
   locator); **NEW SRC** is a work registered in this pass.
3. Quotations in packets are for orientation. Before publishing, recheck wording; enter any ESV quotation in
   `docs/ESV-QUOTATION-LEDGER.md`. Paraphrase-only sources: Turretin (Latin), al-Ghazali *Alchemy* (Field's
   abridged double translation), Bavinck Monergism (compare consequential wording with the Dutch).

## Method

1. Read the reuse map, methodology, source policy, catalog, the Phase 3 dossier and published flagship answers,
   both prior domain dossiers and both registries.
2. For each lane asked what core/primary texts a specialist would expect for ontology, purpose, family, love,
   beauty, creativity and worship; compared with the registry; searched only for named gaps (e.g. "which canonical
   discourse treats duties between parents and children" → DN 31).
3. Read only the needed passages; recorded exact locators.
4. **Mid-run methodological correction (user, 2026-10-06):** for the Reformed lane, Scripture establishes →
   major theologians (Augustine, Calvin, Turretin, Bavinck; then Edwards etc.) explain and defend → confessions
   record the received synthesis. Applied analogously elsewhere: core texts → major interpreters → formal synthesis.
   This led to adding Turretin and mining Bavinck vols. 1–2 and Edwards's *End of Creation* rather than relying on
   confessional chains. Earlier domains were not retrofitted.
5. Stopped each lane when core texts were checked, all five questions had located support, and further reading was
   redundant.

## Important internal distinctions retained

- **Reformed:** broader/narrower image; creation ordinance vs redemptive calling (marriage, singleness); art vs
  image-worship (HC 97; Turretin XI.10); religion vs art (Bavinck I §8.14); regulative principle as Reformed-specific.
- **Naturalism:** explanation vs evaluation; subjectivist vs objectivist meaning; early vs later Russell; byproduct vs
  adaptation; Hume's professed theism.
- **Judaism:** rabbinic praxis vs Maimonidean intellectualism; procreation obligation (man; R. Yohanan b. Beroka;
  Hillel/Shammai; Ben Azzai's exception); covenantal vs universal purpose.
- **Islam:** divine purpose (Ibn Taymiyya vs Ash'ari); love of God as real love vs obedience; contested music;
  marriage normative vs al-Ghazali's weighing.
- **Hindu:** three Vedānta views of self and love; householder vs renouncer; Śaṅkara's ascetic gloss on desire vs
  trivarga.
- **Buddhist:** lay vs monastic; mettā vs attachment; reverence/refuge vs creator-worship; arahant vs bodhisattva.

## Historical and revelatory dependencies (for Revelation & History)

- Reformed and Jewish anthropology depend on Genesis 1–2 as revelation; the historicity of Adam is debated among
  Reformed scholars (flagship disclosure). Islamic anthropology depends on Qur'anic revelation of Adam and the
  *fitra*. Buddhist purpose depends on the Buddha's awakening as a founding event. These are recorded, not tested.
- No tradition here has a single authoritative mechanism for some disputes (e.g. Hindu dharmaśāstra authority;
  Jewish streams on family law)—relevant later to `ultimate-authority`.

## Internal quality pass (single Opus self-review)

- **Core texts before convenience:** every lane's NEW material is primary first (Scripture, Tanakh/Mishnah/Talmud,
  Qur'an/hadith, Upaniṣads/Gītā/Manu, Nikāyas, Darwin/Hume). SEP is used only for debate maps and unreadable CSR.
- **Scripture leads the Reformed lane:** 58 passage readings, each question with a biblical foundation column before
  confession and theologians; after the user's correction, theologians carry explanation in every cell.
- **Family on each tradition's terms:** āśrama and debts (Hindu), lay/monastic and six directions (Buddhist),
  mitzvah and covenant (Jewish), sign and sunna (Islamic), creation ordinance and gift of singleness (Reformed),
  evolutionary function separated from norms (naturalism). No modern nuclear-family template imposed; gender and
  marriage-age provisions flagged for disclosure.
- **Love/beauty/creativity supported, not speculative:** each claim has a text; where none was lawfully available
  (rasa, contemporary aesthetics) the gap is named rather than filled.
- **Worship across theistic and nontheistic lanes:** Buddhist reverence is described through SN 6.2 and DN 16, not as
  creator-worship; naturalist "worship" through Russell and the genealogies, not as disguised religion.
- **No single thinker as tradition:** Maimonides paired with Mishnah/Talmud; al-Ghazali with Qur'an/hadith; Śaṅkara
  with Rāmānuja; Russell with Hume, Darwin and SEP.
- **Selective school use:** Hindu Vedānta three only; Buddhist Pāli baseline with Pure Land/Mahāyāna only for WA/WO.
- **Corrections during the pass:** two unverified Gītā locators (12.13–20, 7.20–23) removed from the Hindu packet;
  Sastri's OCR mislabeling of 10.41 noted; Ormsby and Ghosh scans rejected on copyright grounds; Touger's
