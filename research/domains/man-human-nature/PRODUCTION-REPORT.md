## Domain consistency review (internal, Opus)

All 30 answers were reread together with `great-and-terrible` and `final-end`. No new research occurred in this pass.

1. **Ontology vs flagship:** each lane's `what-is-man` constitution is the same anthropology the flagship uses for greatness and disorder: image and body/soul; evolved organism; composite of earth and breath; clay, spirit, heart and fitra; embodied self in three Vedānta readings; aggregates and conventional person. Moral anthropology is linked, not re-argued.
2. **Purpose vs `final-end`:** Reformed glorifying and enjoying God matches communion; naturalist finite flourishing with no guaranteed end; Jewish service and the World to Come; Islamic worship and love, paradise and vision; Hindu aims and three distinct mokṣa goals; Buddhist noble quest, nirvana and the bodhisattva aim.
3. **Family vs anthropology:** creation ordinance (Reformed), biological explanation without value inference (naturalism), image-linked mitzvah (Judaism), sign and sunna (Islam), householder stage (Hindu) and lay ethic within the noble quest (Buddhism) each follow from the lane's `what-is-man`.
4. **Worship vs ultimate reality:** the theistic lanes worship a personal God; Advaita's two levels and Rāmānuja's eternal devotion are kept distinct; Buddhism has reverence and refuge only; naturalism separates genealogy from evaluation, and Russell's "worship" is marked as early-Russell idealism or a metaphor.
5. **Hindu school identities:** Advaita (Śaṅkara), Viśiṣṭādvaita (Rāmānuja) and Dvaita (Madhva) are consistent across all five questions.
6. **Buddhist no-self:** "not-self," "conventional person" and "conditioned process" are used throughout; no migrating consciousness-substance; no creator-worship.
7. **Jewish strands:** rabbinic composite and praxis vs Maimonidean intellectualism are marked in WM, WA, WO and LB.
8. **Islamic fitra, nafs and purpose:** fitra as orientation or potency (Bukhari 1385; Ibn Taymiyya via Hoover) and heart/nafs (al-Ghazali; 91:7–10) are consistent across WM, WA and WO.
9. **Naturalism:** reductive vs nonreductive, subjectivist vs objectivist, and early vs later Russell are disclosed wherever the position shifts.
10. **Reformed method:** every Christian answer runs Scripture, then theologians (Augustine, Calvin, Turretin, Bavinck, Edwards), then confessional synthesis (WCF/WLC/WSC, Heidelberg, Belgic); no chain of confession citations substitutes for the theologians.
11. **Repetition:** a mechanical check found one repeated 12-word sentence (naturalism WM/WA), which was reworded. No other sentence of 12 or more words repeats across the 30 answers, the flagship or `final-end`.
