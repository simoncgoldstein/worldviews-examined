## How to use

1. **Start with `jesus-evidence.md`** for any JE cell. It is the single shared historical packet:
   - source chronology (§A);
   - the twelve-claim matrix (§B);
   - 1 Cor 15, Galatians, crucifixion, empty tomb, appearances, origin of belief, early Christology, and the resurrection
     inference (§§C–J);
   - the later-claims table (§K).

   Lane packets interpret it; they do not repeat it.
2. Lane packets: `christianity.md`, `naturalism.md`, `judaism.md`, `islam.md`, `hinduism.md`, `buddhism.md`. Each has:
   1. core account;
   2. expected-major-source check;
   3. core texts checked (the Christian packet adds §3a, the Scripture audit);
   4. question map;
   5. passage map;
   6. historical-accountability notes;
   7. internal distinctions;
   8. remaining gaps;
   9. cross-domain consistency.
3. Marks:
   - **REUSE**: established in an earlier pass;
   - **NEW**: read this pass in the registered edition or a same-locator text;
   - **NEW SRC**: registered this pass.
4. Translation discipline:
   - **Paraphrase or our translation only:**
     - Turretin (Latin);
     - Saadia and Nachmanides' *Vikuach* (Hebrew);
     - al-Tabari, al-Razi, Ibn Kathir (Arabic);
     - Ibn Khaldun (de Slane's French);
     - BCA 9.42–44 (Sanskrit).
   - **Bavinck (Monergism):** check the Dutch before quoting consequential wording.
   - **Ledger every ESV quotation.**

## Method

1. **Read first:**
   - the reuse map, METHODOLOGY, SOURCES and the question catalog;
   - the K&T, UR, SD, MH and ME dossiers and the P3 flagship;
   - the published `final-end` and `ultimate-authority` answers;
   - both registries.
2. **Governing order:** core texts → major interpreters → formal synthesis → specialists. For the Reformed lane:
   Scripture → Augustine, Calvin, Turretin, Bavinck → WCF, WLC, Belgic.
3. **Second evidentiary track:** historical claims evaluated historically.
   - Internal authority explains belief; it does not establish history.
   - Every later contrary claim was given its date, kind and earlier source (`jesus-evidence.md` §K).
4. **Narrow, named searches only.** Examples:
   - "Halevi Kuzari I Sefaria Hirschfeld";
   - "classical tafsir Qur'an 4:157 al-Tabari Arabic";
   - "Vikuach HaRamban text";
   - "Dale Martin Yale RLST 152 historical Jesus";
   - "Craig NTS 1985 empty tomb" paired with "Lowder JHC reply".
5. **Source balance for the shared packet:**
   - critical / non-confessional: Martin, Ehrman, Lowder, the Jewish Encyclopedia;
   - defenders: Craig, Habermas, Hurtado;
   - reference: Posen Library, SEP, Birmingham.
6. **Blocked sites not bypassed:**

## History comparison matrix

| Lane | Overall temporal pattern | Is there a goal? | Final horizon | Main internal variation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reformed | Linear, Christ-centred, providential | Yes: God's glory in Christ's kingdom | Resurrection, judgement, renewed creation (re-creation, not annihilation) | Millennial views; the two cities never merge before the end (Augustine) |

| Judaism | Linear covenantal: exile and redemption | Yes: messianic restoration, then the World to Come | Messianic age (this world), resurrection, the World to Come | Maimonides' natural messianic age vs. apocalyptic; calculating the end banned vs. the 6,000-year schema |
| Islam | Linear sacred history with recurring moral rise and fall of peoples | Yes: the Hour and judgement | Resurrection, judgement, Garden or Fire; all perishes but God | Hadith eschatology (Jesus' return, the Mahdi); Ibn Khaldun's political cycles *within* the linear frame |

## Internal quality review (single Opus self-review)

- **Does RV evaluate why a revelation claim should be believed, not merely restate authority?** Yes. Each lane has an
  explicit authentication structure (matrix) separate from its UA final court:
  - Reformed: three levels;
  - Judaism: shared witness, not wonders;
  - Islam: *iʿjāz* vs al-Ghazali's converging evidence;
  - Hindu: four school-specific answers;
  - Buddhist: experiential verification and the Mahāyāna parity argument;
  - naturalism: Hume and the Bayesian map.
- **Did every lane begin from its own foundational texts?** Yes:
  - Reformed: Scripture audit first;
  - Judaism: Exodus and Deuteronomy;
  - Islam: the Qur'an's challenge verses;
  - Hindu: BS and the Gītā;
  - Buddhist: the Nikāyas;
  - naturalism: Hume as the classical text, as the lane has no canon.
- **Are public historical claims separated from internal authority claims?** Yes. Every lane packet has a §6
  historical-accountability section, and the README matrix has a separate "public/historical component" column.
- **Is the historical Jesus evidence common across lanes?** Yes. One packet; lanes cite its sections.
- **Are source dates and proximity accurate?** Only dates found in read sources are stated as such. Others are flagged
  as standard estimates (Tacitus, Pliny) or ranges (Gospels). Josephus is self-dated (*Ant.* XX.11.3).
- **Is the crucifixion represented at the strength of the evidence?** Yes: "strongly established", with Paul, the Gospels
  (including the independent titulus), Tacitus and the Josephus core. The Qur'anic denial is presented as revelation.
- **Are appearances distinguished from the resurrection inference?** Yes (§B 6 vs 11; §G).
- **Is the empty tomb a real debate?** Yes. Craig (NTS) vs Lowder (JHC) at full strength; Habermas's majority claim
  attributed as an apologist's survey; Lowder's own concession (slightly better than even odds) recorded.
- **Is early Christology dated cautiously?** Yes. "Within two decades" for worship (Hurtado); Phil 2 pre-Pauline only
  "perhaps"; Rom 9:5 punctuation flagged; no Nicaea-from-a-hymn.
- **Is Qur'an 4:157 represented accurately?** Yes. Arabic checked. "Made to appear so to them". No named substitute in the
  text. Three classical tafsirs with conflicting substitution stories; al-Razi's objection; modern minority readings.
- **Are later Islamic, Jewish, Hindu and Buddhist claims weighted by provenance?** Yes (§K). The Qur'anic and Talmudic
  claims contradict earlier evidence without earlier sources. Hindu and Buddhist receptions mostly reinterpret meaning.
- **Does Sinai get both its steelman and historical evaluation?** Yes. Halevi I.8 and I.87; Maimonides YT 8; Gottlieb's
  modern principle; Tal's critique; Farber's critical evaluation. The same standard is applied to Calvin I.8.5.
- **Does *iʿjāz* get both?** Yes. The Qur'an's challenge and the Muslim doctor's statement; al-Ghazali's internal
  qualification; Halevi's critique; preservation kept separate from inspiration.
- **Are Vedic preservation and divine origin kept distinct?** Yes. Hindu §6: "accurately preserved, therefore revealed"
  is ruled out.
- **Is Buddhist revelation terminology comparative, not imposed?** Yes. "Disclosure", "discovery" and "Buddha-word"
  throughout. The SN 12.65 discovery framing leads.
- **Does HI stay about history as a whole, not repeat `final-end`?** Yes. Each cell treats cosmic and historical pattern
  (cycles, eons, sacred history, dynastic cycles, progress) and links to final-end only for the personal goal.
- **Are all 18 grades honest?** Yes. Islam JE is graded Ready because the named gap (4:157) is closed at primary level.
  Every other JE cell carries the shared caveat. No cell is graded Ready on a secondary source where a primary was
  required.

2. Do not let the JE historical comparison become the whole answer for the Hindu and Buddhist lanes, whose receptions
   mostly do not dispute the history.
